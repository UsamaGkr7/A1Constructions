About A1 Constructions

A family construction business, run the way you'd build your own home.

A1 Constructions is a full-service construction company handling homes, villas, interiors, exteriors and swimming pools — led personally by Mushtaq Gangrekar and a small crew of experienced site engineers and craftsmen.

We stay on-site through every stage, from the first excavation to the final coat of paint, so nothing gets lost in translation between the plan and what actually gets built.

Transparent costing

Clear, itemised quotes with no hidden charges added later.

How we work
